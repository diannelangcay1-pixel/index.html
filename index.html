About MindCare 💜

MindCare is a student wellness space designed to encourage emotional awareness, reflection, and healthy calming habits.

Your privacy matters

Do not enter your full name, school ID, address, passwords, or other identifying information in wellness activities.

Need support?

MindCare is not a substitute for professional help. If you're struggling, consider reaching out to a trusted adult, school counselor, or qualified mental health professional.
