A LITTLE ABOUT THIS SPACE

Welcome to MindCare 💜

MindCare is a student wellness space for reflection, emotional awareness, and gentle self-care.

Your privacy matters

Journal entries on this version stay in the browser on the device where they are saved. They are not a secure, encrypted diary. Avoid writing passwords or identifying details. Clearing browser data may delete your entries.

A gentle reminder

MindCare is here to support reflection and self-care, but it is not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you're struggling, consider reaching out to a trusted adult, school counselor, or qualified professional.
